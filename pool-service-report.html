The three most expensive words in pool service

'You skipped us.' Without a record, that conversation is your word against a memory — and it gets worse every summer. A dated report with the day's readings, what you dosed, what you cleaned and what the water looked like after ends it.

What's inside every report

Date and visit summary; free chlorine, pH, TA and CYA as logged at the water; chemicals added with amounts; your checklist (skim, brush, vacuum, baskets — or your own list); notes; and before/after photos straight from your camera roll. One tap exports a PDF you can send from Messages, Mail or WhatsApp.

For owners who want receipts, not invoices

Full billing software (job management, invoicing, payments) exists — and it costs a monthly office tax you may not want yet. This is the record layer: proof of service and water history, per pool, in your pocket. Add your invoices later from the same source of truth.

Questions pool techs ask

What goes in the PDF service report?

Date, water readings (FC, pH, TA, CYA), chemicals added with amounts, completed checklist tasks, notes and before/after photos for that visit.

How do I send it to customers?

One tap shares the PDF to any app — Messages, Mail, WhatsApp. No email server, no customer login, no portal for them to forget.

Do customers need the app too?

No. They get the PDF; you keep the history. The report is plain enough for the least technical homeowner.

Is it an invoicing app?

It's a proof-of-service and history app. Many solo techs pair it with whatever they already use for billing — the chemical record and photos live here.
