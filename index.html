Everything a Route Needs

The five things you actually do on a route — scheduled, recorded and provable.

🗓️

Plan Your Week

Assign pools to weekdays with a service time and duration, see the whole route at a glance, and add a one-off visit when a customer calls. Your recurring route stays organised week after week.

🧪

Log Real Readings

Record free chlorine, pH, total alkalinity and CYA at the pool edge, plus every chemical you added. Numbers go into the pool's history instead of a notebook you'll never open again.

✅

Your Own Checklist

Skim, brush, vacuum, empty baskets — tick the tasks you actually perform, or build your own checklist to match how you work. Nothing gets forgotten mid-route.

📄

PDF Service Reports

Turn a completed visit into a clean PDF report with readings, chemicals, tasks, notes and before/after photos. Send it to the customer and the "did you even come?" conversation ends.

📍

Drive Straight There

Every pool carries its address, gate and access notes, phone number and service history. Tap the address to open it in Apple Maps and head to your next stop.

🔒

No Account, Works Offline

Your pool and customer records stay on your device. No sign-up, no dashboard to configure, no monthly software bill. It works at the back of a yard with no signal.

Built for Solo Techs and Small Operators

Most pool software is priced and designed for companies with an office. You drive the route yourself.

Ditch the Notebook and the Group Chat

No more paper route sheets, spreadsheets, or photos dumped in a messaging thread. Every pool, reading and visit lives in one app you already have in your pocket.

Proof You Were There

Before and after photos with the date, the chemicals you added and the tasks you completed. When a customer questions a visit, you send the report instead of arguing.

History That Answers Questions

"When did we last shock this pool?" Open the pool and its whole service history is there — including gate codes and equipment notes you'd otherwise relearn every season.

Fast Because You're On Your Feet

Logging a visit takes seconds, one-handed, standing next to the water. That is the whole design brief — no forms, no required fields, no training.
